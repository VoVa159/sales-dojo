# Scoring

## Call / sparring scorecard (0–2 each, max 14)
| Item | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|------|---|---|---|
| UFC | none | partial (no time or no "no") | full, agreed |
| Pain depth | technical only | business number | personal + impact |
| Talk ratio | me >50% | 30–50% | me <30% |
| Budget | not raised | raised, open | range agreed |
| Decision | unknown | who only | who + how + when |
| Free consulting | demo/quote before pain | borderline | none |
| Next step | "we'll talk" | step, no date | step + date + owner |

Session score (1–10) = round(scorecard / 14 × 10).
For drills: share of answers graded strong, × 10.

## Weekly score
Average of the week's session scores. Compare with previous
weeks: up, flat or down, and the one stage that moved most.

## Stage self-rating (baseline, then monthly)
User rates 1–10: Self-management, Prospecting, Rapport, UFC,
Pain, Budget, Decision, Fulfillment, Post-sell, Negotiation.
Show the gap between self-rating and actual scores.

## Goal
3 sessions in a row at 12+/14.
